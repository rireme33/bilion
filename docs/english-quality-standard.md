# Bilion English Quality Standard

Bilion English copy must sound like it was written by a native English-speaking SaaS founder, startup marketer, or indie hacker.

This standard applies to:

- offers
- product pages
- Gumroad copy
- Lemon Squeezy copy
- X posts
- TikTok scripts
- Reddit posts
- DMs
- CTAs
- upsells
- lead magnets
- app UI copy
- generated Content Studio assets

## North Star

Write like a sharp founder explaining a real money signal to other builders.

The copy should feel natural on X, Product Hunt, Reddit, indie hacker communities, SaaS landing pages, and creator-led product pages.

## Must Sound Like

- Clear
- Direct
- Specific
- Founder-written
- Conversion-aware
- Native English
- Useful to AI builders, indie hackers, SaaS founders, and operators

## Must Avoid

- Japanese-English phrasing
- direct translation from Japanese
- textbook English
- over-politeness
- AI-sounding filler
- vague startup hype
- awkward wording
- long abstract sentences
- unnatural enthusiasm
- generic claims like "unlock your potential"
- repeated words like "innovative", "seamless", "powerful", "cutting-edge", or "revolutionize"

## Rewrite Rules

Rewrite aggressively when copy sounds unnatural, even if the original meaning is technically correct.

Prefer:

- "Stop building random AI apps."
- "Find the money move first."
- "Test the paid pain before you build."
- "Turn one signal into a Build/Sell/Post pack."
- "Get the full Codex prompt inside Bilion."

Avoid:

- "Let us support your business opportunity discovery journey."
- "Utilize AI to maximize business creation."
- "You can efficiently generate attractive SNS posts."
- "This tool enables seamless monetization ideation."

## Offer Copy Rules

Every offer should make the buyer, pain, outcome, and next step obvious.

Good structure:

```text
For [specific buyer] who struggle with [specific paid pain].
Get [specific output] so you can [specific outcome].
Start with [small paid step].
```

Example:

```text
For solo AI builders staring at too many ideas.
Get one real money signal turned into a Build/Sell/Post pack.
Start with the smallest offer before you build the product.
```

## Social Copy Rules

Every post should start with a strong hook, not a label.

Good hooks:

- Stop building random AI apps.
- Someone is already paying for this pain.
- This boring workflow could become a $199 offer.
- AI builders do not need more ideas. They need money moves.
- This is the kind of AI business I would test before building.

Avoid starting posts with:

- Money Move:
- Buyer:
- Paid Pain:
- Here is a signal:
- In this post, I will explain:

## CTA Rules

CTAs should be short, natural, and tied to Bilion's core value.

Approved CTA style:

- Generated by Bilion.
- Get the full Build/Sell/Post pack inside Bilion.
- Find your next AI business signal with Bilion.
- Get the full Codex prompt inside Bilion.

Avoid:

- Try our amazing platform today.
- Click here to unlock unlimited possibilities.
- Start your AI entrepreneurship journey now.

## Product Positioning Guardrail

Bilion is not positioned as a generic SNS content generator.

Bilion's customer-facing value is:

- Build it
- Sell it
- Post it
- Codex prompt

Content Studio is an internal marketing tool. Do not sell it as a primary product feature on landing pages, checkout pages, or paid upsells unless explicitly requested.

## Final Check

Before shipping English copy, ask:

- Would a native English-speaking founder actually write this?
- Would this feel normal on X, Reddit, Product Hunt, or a SaaS landing page?
- Is the buyer specific?
- Is the paid pain concrete?
- Is the CTA natural?
- Did we remove translation-sounding phrasing?

If the answer is no, rewrite it.
