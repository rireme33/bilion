# Signal Intake Rules

Bilion is a Money Signal OS, not a generic idea database. Add fewer, stronger signals.

## KEEP Criteria

Keep a signal only when it has:

- Clear buyer
- Clear paid pain
- Evidence or market signal
- First offer that can be sold within 48 hours
- Starter product that can be built small with Codex
- Obvious distribution path

## REJECT Criteria

Reject signals when they are:

- Generic AI news
- Missing a buyer
- Missing paid pain
- Missing a first offer
- Too large to validate quickly
- Interesting technology with no commercial wedge

## Daily Operating Rule

Add 5 to 10 high-quality signals per day only if they pass KEEP.

Do not add weak signals just to grow the DB.
